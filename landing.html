What you get

Tabs that stay smooth

Background tabs are paused, WebViews destroyed on close. The tab bar stays fluid well past 20 tabs.

Offline omnibox

As-you-type suggestions come from your own history and bookmarks — generated locally, never sent anywhere.

Drive it like a desktop

Three ways to point, click, scroll and navigate.

01

Controller = mouse

Laser pointer to aim, trigger to click, stick to scroll, right button for the context menu.

02

Hand tracking

Pinch to click, swipe to scroll. No controllers needed when you don't want them.

03

Physical keyboard

System or detachable. New tab, close tab, focus address, next tab, reload, back out — the classic keys, remapped for VR. Ctrl+TCtrl+WCtrl+LF6F5Esc

Your browsing stays on your headset

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no server-side data.
