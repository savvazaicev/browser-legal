VR Browser 2D Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-12

This privacy policy applies to the VR Browser 2D app (hereby referred to as "Application") for Meta Quest devices that was created by Savva (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. It does not collect, sell, or share any personal information. All of your data is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to the Service Provider or any third party. Because the Application is a web browser, the data it keeps is the data a browser normally keeps for you:

  • Your browsing history — the addresses, titles and times of the pages you visit
  • Your bookmarks (the pages you choose to save)
  • Cookies, cache and per-site web storage (such as localStorage) for the sites you visit, used by those sites
  • Your restored tab state — the tabs you had open when you last closed the Application
  • Your app settings — theme, start page, Ad Blocker settings, screen size preset, and other preferences

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your VR device, does not access your contacts, camera, or microphone, and does not use advertising identifiers. It makes no network calls at startup.

The address bar and searching

The address bar (omnibox) offers suggestions as you type. These suggestions are generated locally from your own history and bookmarks and are never sent to any server. When you enter a full web address, the Application navigates to it directly. When you enter a search term (not a web address) and submit it, that term is sent to the search engine you use to retrieve its public results — exactly as it would be in any browser. No other data, no identifiers, and no information about your other browsing are sent with the query.

Ad Blocker (EasyList)

The built-in Ad Blocker filters requests using the public, community-maintained EasyList ruleset. All filtering happens entirely on your device. The Application does not send your browsing activity, your addresses, or any information about you to the ad-list maintainer or any other third party. (If you allow the ruleset to update, the update request carries no personal data.) You can switch the Ad Blocker off at any time in the settings.

Cookies, cache and site data

When you visit websites, they may set cookies and store data, and the Application keeps that data locally (in the browser's standard cookie, cache and web storage) the same way a desktop browser does. This data belongs to the sites you visit and is governed by their own privacy policies. You can delete individual history entries and bookmarks at any time, and uninstalling the Application removes all locally stored cookies, cache, and browsing data with it.

Optional Meta telemetry

The Application uses the Meta Spatial SDK. Meta may offer optional, opt-in diagnostics/telemetry for Meta SDKs; this is governed by Meta's own privacy policy and terms (see Meta Privacy Policy). Telemetry is disabled unless you opt in through the system or SDK prompts, and opting in is not required to use the Application. The Service Provider does not receive, access, or review any telemetry data.

Permissions

The Application requests only the Internet permission, which it needs to load the web pages you navigate to. It requests no other runtime permissions.

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not share, sell, or transmit any of your personal information to third parties.

Please note that the Application is a web browser. When you visit a website, that website receives the same information that any browser would send (for example, the request for a page and your IP address). This is inherent to using the web and is not something the Application adds; each website's own privacy policy governs what it collects. The only other outbound requests the Application makes are the search queries you submit, the optional Ad Blocker ruleset update, and the optional Meta SDK telemetry described above (handled entirely by Meta, and only if you opt in).

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your VR device or via the VR application marketplace or network. Uninstalling the Application permanently removes your history, bookmarks, cookies, cache, tab state, and settings from the device. You can also reduce any network use by switching off the Ad Blocker auto-update, by not using the address bar for searches, and by not opting in to Meta SDK telemetry (which you can disable in the Meta system settings; the Service Provider does not control or receive that data).

Data Retention Policy

All Application data (history, bookmarks, cookies, cache, tab state, and settings) is stored only on your device and is retained only as long as the Application is installed. The Service Provider has no server-side copies of your data and cannot access your data. If you delete the Application, your local data is deleted with it.

Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. Because the Application does not collect any personal information of its own, the Service Provider has no such data to delete; if you believe a child has used the Application, please contact the Service Provider at oisurka@gmail.com so that they can take any necessary actions.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application stores your data only on your device and transmits nothing off-device except the web pages you navigate to, the search queries you submit, and the optional items described above, there is no Service Provider-side storage to secure. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards for any information they process or maintain.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
